## What most outlets wait for

The same few things open almost every door, in this order: the name (Q1); a demo video that shows a real reaction; photos of the tag on a handle; the compliance mark and the battery statement, which are engineering's rows; packaging; a barcode. The design proposal decides how the photos and the packaging look.
