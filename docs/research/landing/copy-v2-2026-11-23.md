## 1. Audit of the copy as it was

**Behaviour wanted.** A maker, first-time founder or small product firm (who) sends the two-field form (action) in the hero, pricing or close block (where). Done = a claimed early-access place.

**Binding barrier: motivation.** Ability is fine: two fields, no card, one action, a form in the first screen. The prompt is fine: the action is in the header at every width. What fails is the reason to act. The old first screen named a tool ("One workbench") and a promise the proof could not carry ("built and on sale"), and gave no number, no time and no feeling the visitor would recognise.

### What is not claimed, and why

- **"About $6 a unit at 100 units"** (from the brief). The example's rows give $17.67 a unit at 100 and an own-site net of $21.95, which leaves $4.28. $5.95 is the figure at 1,000 units ($16.00, itself an assumption). The page says "about $4 a unit" at 100.
- **"In a weekend" as a promise to the visitor.** It is one run by the builder. It appears only as "our first idea" and "one of us".
- **Any time saving, "faster", or a comparison with agencies.** Never measured; no sourced figure.
- **"Built", "on sale", "launched".** The example is designed and costed. The headline now promises the process, and the status line says where the example stands.
- **Customers, testimonials, logos, counts.** None exist.
- **That a product will sell.** FAQ 7 says the opposite in plain words.
- **Live integrations.** FAQ 7 keeps "tested against stand-ins".
- **Ownership of ideas.** Not ruled. Only the owner's sentence on who can see an idea.
- The words free, demo, self-host, open source, GitHub: absent, and the tests still enforce it.

One caution for the owner: the hero's picture still runs its line to a pill that reads "On sale". That is the picture, not the copy, so it was left alone, but it now sits beside a caption that says "not yet built or sold".

## 6. Three tests, by traffic source

No lift is predicted. Each needs the funnel counts in Admin (visits and form sends by placement) and a way to serve two headlines, which the page does not have yet.

1. **Maker communities and hardware forums (people who already build).** Headline 1 against headline 2 ("Know what your product costs before you spend."). Hypothesis: people who have already paid for a bad first batch respond to the costing more than to "to market". Measure hero form sends per visit. Guardrail: the share of "What do you want to make?" answers that name a physical product.
2. **Founders arriving from a share of the film or the story (social, the owner's own posts).** Film block second, as now, against the proof block second with the film after it. Hypothesis: these visitors came for the joke and need the numbers sooner. Measure reach of `#proof` and form sends from the proof and close placements.
3. **Search visitors on cost and pricing terms ("what does it cost to make a product", "BOM cost calculator").** Headline 5 with the close block's text as the subhead, against headline 1. Hypothesis: a question that matches the search converts better than a statement. Measure hero form sends per visit, and time to first scroll past the hero.

For how many visits each test needs, use a sample-size tool; with 50 places the tests will mostly run on the waiting list.
