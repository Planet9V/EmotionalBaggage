# BAGGAGE CLAIMS MK1 — MARKETING CAMPAIGN PLAYBOOK (Rev A, 19 Nov 2026)
# Goal hierarchy: (1) Phase-1 gate: 50k organic views or 500 signups on $0-500.
# (2) Kickstarter: 2,000 backers, CAC < $8, conv > 2.5%. (3) Post-campaign
# sell-through + 20% DLC voice-pack attach. Budget aligns to PRD: $3k creator
# seeding + $2k amplification (only on proven organic clips).
# Brand voice rules: the BAG speaks first person, never the company. Deadpan
# host, chaotic bag. Every asset ends with ONE action. No celebrity voices.

FUNNEL STAGE 1 — ATTENTION (spend: $0–500, creator seeding only)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CAMPAIGN 1: "THE FOUR GATES" — unbranded proof-of-funny drops
Format: the 4 verticals from the launch script (FAT / OW / DARK / WHEE),
posted raw, no logo, no link, morning commuter slots (6–8am local).
Hooks (on-screen text):
 - "POV: your suitcase gets body-shamed at check-in"   (weigh-in)
 - "the suitcase screamed and the baggage handler LOST IT" (toss)
 - "my luggage after 4 hours in the cargo hold"             (dark)
 - "why is this bag having more fun than me"                (carousel)
Comment trap: pinned reply "comment FAT for where to get one" — keyword
auto-DM via ManyChat to a one-field email capture. This is the entire
Phase-1 gate. Kill/scale rule: any clip >50k views in 72h gets the $2k
amplification split 70/30 to that platform.

CAMPAIGN 2: "@TheBagComplains" — the character account
The bag runs its own TikTok/X/IG. It replies IN CHARACTER to viral travel
complaint posts ("crew rested me on the WET tarmac?? sir I am luggage").
Daypart: replies to airline meltdown posts within 30 min of virality.
Bio is the only link out: email capture. Budget: $0; cost is wit + speed.
KPI: 3 reply screenshots reposted by travel accounts >100k followers.

CAMPAIGN 3: "100 BAGS, 100 REACTIONS" — creator seeding
Ship 20 evaluation units to mid-size travel comedians (50k–500k followers,
NOT mega-influencers — mid-tier has 3x the engagement rate for props).
Deal structure: free unit + affiliate code worth 10% of pledged value,
zero creative brief except "film the first genuine reaction."
Stack the feed: all 20 post within the launch week so the algorithm reads
"travel bag screaming" as a trend, not an ad. Budget: 20 units ≈ $1.3k COGS.

FUNNEL STAGE 2 — INTEREST → CAPTURE (spend: ~$500 tooling)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CAMPAIGN 4: "THE LOST BAGGAGE HOTLINE" — interactive microsite
URL: BaggageClaims.com (or SassCase.com — pending name decision).
Fake customer-service flow: "File a complaint on behalf of your bag."
User answers 3 questions (where did your bag suffer? what did they put you
through? rate the trauma), site generates a personalized audio complaint
IN THE BAG'S VOICE + a shareable "CLAIM TICKET" image (joke legal doc:
"Claimant alleges being called FAT at Terminal C...").
Trade: ticket is free; downloading/Sharing unlocks with email. Monthly
best complaint wins a free unit — submissions become Phase-2 content.
KPI: email capture rate >35% of visitors; 1,000 tickets pre-launch.

CAMPAIGN 5: "THE DEPARTURE BOARD" — pre-launch referral program
Email capture page styled as an airport departure board; your position on
the board IS your referral rank. Mechanics:
 - 1 signup = "BOARDING GROUP D"
 - 3 referrals = free voice pack at launch ("upgrade to Group B")
 - 10 referrals = Early Bird $19 price LOCKED ("Group A")
 - 25 referrals = collector Gold Voice tier ("FIRST CLASS")
Airport metaphors carry the mechanics; every share message is pre-written
as a complaint from the user's bag. KPI: 3,000 emails before Kickstarter
launch (industry rule: 1–3% of list converts; 3,000 × 2% × $24 ≈ $1.4k
day-one velocity, which triggers Kickstarter's trending algorithm).

FUNNEL STAGE 3 — CONVERSION (spend: Kickstarter page + $1k assets)

CAMPAIGN 6: "THE COMPLAINT DESK" — live stunt (Austin home-field)
Pop-up mock airline complaint desk at a high-foot-traffic Austin venue
(time against SXSW or a travel expo; the PRD already assumes local shoots).
A "claims agent" in uniform takes REAL travelers' baggage horror stories;
the bag unit on the counter reacts to each with a live gag; claimant gets
a printed claim ticket with a QR code = Kickstarter secret reward tier
(desk-only $18 "Gate Agent Special", 200 units, artificial scarcity).
Film everything — this generates the launch-week content reservoir.
Budget: ~$300 (banner, tickets, blazer). KPI: 500 QR scans, 2 local
news hits ("a suitcase filed a complaint at the airport today").

CAMPAIGN 7: KICKSTARTER PAGE — comedy-native conversion architecture
Video: the 2:15 hero cut (already scripted). Page structure:
 - Headline: "It knows when it's being weighed. And it is NOT happy."
 - Tier ladder as cabin classes: CHECKED $19 (early bird, 300 cap) /
   CARRY-ON $24 / OVERHEAD BIN $34 (2-pack) / FIRST CLASS $49 collector
   (Gold Voice, signed, 100 cap) / THE WHOLE CARGO HOLD $99 (4-pack —
   the groomsmen/secret-santa bundle; 4-packs are where margin lives).
 - Stretch goals as voice packs, not hardware: $25k unlocks International
   pack (multilingual complaints), $40k unlocks Holiday Heckler pack.
   Stretch goals that cost $0.50/unit to fulfill = pure margin retention.
 - The 10%-of-backers rule: the $49 tier exists to capture the whale 10%
   who bring 60% of funds. Never cap it below demand signal.
 - Add-on engine: $7.99 DLC voice packs at checkout (microSD, 50c BOM).
FAQ written in-character: "Q: Will TSA confiscate it? A: Alkaline AA.
No lithium. We checked. The bag insists you switch it OFF before checking."

CAMPAIGN 8: RETARGETING — "THE SHAME ADS"
Ads to video-viewers who didn't convert, written as 5-star complaint reviews:
 - "My bag embarrassed me in front of the entire carousel. Five stars."
 - "TSA agent laughed so hard she forgot to swab my laptop. Five stars."
 - "Ruined my marriage. She took his side. The bag's side. Five stars."
Static image + one gag audio autoplay-off. $500 test, kill if CAC > $10.

FUNNEL STAGE 4 — RETENTION / FLYWHEEL (post-fulfillment)

CAMPAIGN 9: "PLATINUM STATUS" — the DLC flywheel
Every unit ships with a "frequent flyer card" (QR): register unit = free
first DLC voice pack; seasonal packs drop quarterly (Holiday Heckler,
Summer Breakdown, Bachelor Party Layover) at $7.99. Anniversary event:
"WORST FLIGHT STORY" contest — winner's true story is recorded by the
voice talent as a limited voice pack; every entrant produces UGC; winner
produces press. Target: 20% of buyers attach ≥1 DLC in 12 months, and
every contest cycle re-feeds Stage-1 content for free.

CAMPAIGN 10: WHOLESALE TROJAN — the counter display
Airport gift retail (post-campaign): counter display with a big red button
— press it, the display unit plays one random gag. It demos itself at the
point of maximum despair (the airport). Wholesale $14.50 vs $29.95 MSRP
gives retailers 2x markup; the button converts the browser so the
retailer never has to sell. Pitch line to buyers: "the only product in
your store that demos itself to people who are already mad about bags."
