# Scope: public front end (brand + funnel) and backend management for the
# full lifecycle: pre-launch capture -> Kickstarter -> DTC store -> DLC flywheel.
# Feeds: Marketing Playbook Rev A campaigns 1-10; PRD channel economics.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. STRATEGY — ONE PLATFORM, FOUR PERSONALITIES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
The site is not a brochure; it is the fourth character in the brand
(host, agent, bag, WEBSITE). Design principle: every page masquerades as
a piece of airport infrastructure — departure board, scale readout,
complaint form, frequent-flyer portal — while every masquerade ends in
exactly one conversion action.

Funnel mapping (playbook -> site surface):
 - Four Gates / character account  -> Landing w/ email capture ("Gate")
 - Lost Baggage Hotline            -> Interactive complaint generator
 - Departure Board referral        -> Referral status board
 - Kickstarter                     -> Bridge page + post-KS migration
 - Shame ads retargeting           -> Product page w/ social proof wall
 - Platinum Status DLC             -> Customer portal (registration, packs)
 - Wholesale Trojan                -> Retail/wholesale lead form

2. TECH STACK (phased, priced Nov 2026)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CORE DECISION: Shopify from day one as the system of record — it runs the
pre-launch landing + later store without replatforming.
 - Shopify Basic $39/mo (or $29/mo annual; new stores: 3 days free then
   $1/mo for 3 months) + Shopify Payments 2.9%+30c online.
 - Theme: free Dawn, heavily customized Liquid sections; no paid theme.
 - Email/SMS: Klaviyo — free tier 250 profiles/500 sends, then $20/mo
   (500 profiles) -> ~$100/mo at 5k. Watch: bills on ACTIVE profiles.
 - Referral engine: Viral Loops Start-up $49/mo (1,000 participants) for
   the pre-launch window ONLY; cancel after Kickstarter; native
   Shopify Flow + custom codes thereafter.
 - Landing micro-apps (Hotline, Departure Board): Next.js + Vercel free
   tier, serverless functions, Supabase free tier (Postgres) for
   tickets/referrals pre-Shopify; embed as Shopify pages via iframe/
   section. Cost ~$0 until traffic >100k hits/mo.
 - Reviews/UGC: Judge.me free tier. Chat: Shopify Inbox free.
 - Analytics: GA4 + Shopify analytics + Meta/TikTok pixels. Heatmaps:
   Microsoft Clarity (free).
 - Domains: brand .com + sasscase.com redirect (~$25/yr both).
STACK COST: Phase 1 ~ $90/mo | Post-KS ~ $140/mo | Scale (5k list) ~ $240/mo

4. FRONT-END PAGE DESIGN (the clever layer)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 GATE (homepage)
 - Full-viewport departure board (split-flap animation, CSS/JS) cycling:
   "AUS → FAT JOKES — ON TIME", "DFW → YOUR BAG — DELAYED (AGAIN)",
   "BAG → COMPLAINTS — NOW BOARDING".
 - The hero video muted-autoplays in a CRT "security monitor" frame.
 - Single CTA styled as a check-in kiosk button: "CHECK MY BAG IN"
   (email field). Below fold: 3-panel moments strip, press logos,
   compliance badge strip ("AA-powered. No lithium. Airline-legit.").
 - Easter egg: idle 20s and the page itself complains (audio opt-in).

4.2 SAFETY CARD (/how-it-works)
 - Airline safety-card illustration language: 6 frames from strap-on to
   carousel gag; zero paragraphs; ends in CTA. Doubles as packaging art
   (one asset, two uses).

4.3 LOST BAGGAGE HOTLINE (/hotline)  [the viral engine]
 - Step 1: "Where did your bag suffer?" (visual airport map picker)
 - Step 2: "What did they do to it?" (checkboxes with absurd options)
 - Step 3: "Rate the trauma" (slider: 'shook' -> 'scarred')
 - Output: personalized 10s audio complaint in the bag's voice (TTS
   pre-rendered fragments stitched server-side) + "CLAIM TICKET" image
   (faux legal doc with claim number). Download/share unlocks with email.
 - Leaderboard: "Complaint of the Week" — monthly winner gets free unit.
 - Rate limit + captcha; moderation queue before anything appears publicly.

4.4 DEPARTURE BOARD (/board)
 - User's referral rank rendered as a boarding group with live split-flap
   updates; milestone toasts ("You've been upgraded to GROUP B").
 - Share cards auto-generated with the user's claim number and rank.
 - Fraud: 1 referral per verified email; IP+device heuristics via
   Viral Loops built-in fraud flags, manual review at reward issuance.

4.5 PRODUCT PAGE (/product)
 - Styled as an airline SPEC SHEET placard; price shown as a scale
   readout ("$24.95 — UNDER THE LIMIT").
 - Reviews rendered as 5-star complaint tickets (matches Shame Ads).
 - Bundle builder: 1/2/4-pack ("Overhead Bin" / "Whole Cargo Hold") with
   per-unit savings; voice-pack add-on selector in-cart ($7.99).
 - Compliance card PDF download + "OFF before checking" safety video.

4.6 PLATINUM STATUS (/claim)
 - QR on the in-box frequent-flyer card lands here: enter unit serial
   (printed on compliance card) -> free DLC voice pack + registration.
 - Registration captures: email, purchase channel, unit serial, travel
   frequency — this is your post-purchase segmentation goldmine.

4.7 COMPLAINT DEPARTMENT (/faq)
 - All support in character ("Our agents are currently on a tarmac
   delay"); real answers underneath every joke. Self-serve order lookup
   (Shopify native), returns per policy, safety/compliance links.

5. BACKEND MANAGEMENT (admin surfaces & responsibilities)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 MODULES
 - ORDERS: Shopify admin (native) — KS pledge import via CSV mapping
   tier->SKU; tags: ks_checked, ks_firstclass, gate_agent_special.
 - DLC FULFILLMENT: voice packs = Shopify digital products w/ physical
   microSD option; serial-keyed download (Sky Pilot app ~$5-15/mo or
   custom S3-signed links via Supabase function).
 - REGISTRATION DB (Supabase): units(serial, batch, mfg_date),
   registrations(serial->email, channel, travel_freq, ts). Powers
   warranty, DLC entitlement, and recall/safety comms (compliance-grade).
 - REFERRALS: Viral Loops (pre-KS) -> export -> Shopify customer tags
   + discount codes post-KS (single migration runbook).
 - EMAIL/SEGMENTS: Klaviyo lists: gate_signup, hotline_user, backer,
   buyer, registered, dlc_buyer, wholesale_lead. Flows: welcome (3-email
   in-character sequence), abandon-cart, post-purchase (day-10: register
   unit; day-30: review ask; day-45: DLC upsell), winback (90d).
 - AFFILIATES: creator codes as Shopify discount codes w/ 10% payout
   tracked via monthly report export (defer affiliate SaaS until >$20k/mo).
 - UGC MODERATION: Hotline submissions + contest entries queue; approve/
   feature toggle feeds /stories wall. Voice-release checkbox at submit.
 - SUPPORT: Shopify Inbox + macros for top-5 issues (see build guide
   troubleshooting sheet — same root causes).
 - WHOLESALE CRM: leads in Klaviyo (wholesale_lead list) + downloadable
   line sheet behind form; follow-up sequence = 3 emails + sample offer.

5.2 DATA MODEL (core entities)
 customer(id, email, source, ks_tier, tags[])
 unit(serial, batch, sku, mfg_date)
 registration(unit_id, customer_id, channel, travel_freq, created_at)
 referral(referrer_customer, referee_email, milestone, status, ts)
 claim_ticket(id, email, location, insult, trauma, audio_url, moderated)
 voice_pack(sku, name, folder_map, price, active)
 entitlement(customer_id, voice_pack_sku, source, ts)
 wholesale_lead(company, channel_type, est_doors, contact, stage)

5.3 KEY FLOWS (automation)
 - KS import: backer CSV -> create orders (pre-paid flag) -> tag by tier
   -> Klaviyo backer list -> fulfillment batch by region.
 - Register unit: serial validated against units table -> entitlement
   free_pack granted -> Klaviyo 'registered' + DLC cross-sell in 14 days.
 - Hotline submit -> moderation -> on approve: public wall + Klaviyo
   'hotline_user' + weekly digest entry.
 - Shame-ad click -> /product w/ UTM -> view-content pixel event ->
   7-day retargeting audience; conversion breaks loop.

6. COMPLIANCE & OPS (non-negotiables)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 - Privacy: GDPR + CCPA. Cookie consent banner (geo-aware), DSAR delete
   via Shopify native tools + Supabase row wipe runbook. NL/EU audience
   = strict GDPR defaults; do NOT gate the Hotline behind consent walls,
   just collect less.
 - Email: CAN-SPAM/GDPR double opt-in for EU IPs; one-click unsub.
 - UGC: submission checkbox grants reuse license; celebrity-voice ban
   enforced in moderation; minors' content auto-rejected (age attest).
 - Payments: Shopify Payments (PCI handled). No card data touches
   Supabase ever.
 - Safety/compliance page hosts the FAA PackSafe rationale + "switch OFF
   before checking" card (same PDF as in-box).
 - Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA on core flows (contrast, keyboard, alt) —
   gag site is no excuse; lawsuits target novelty brands too.
 - Uptime/scale: all static/CDN; Supabase free->Pro $25/mo at >500MB;
   Vercel free->Pro $20/mo at high traffic. Failure budget: Hotline down
   = email capture still works (static fallback form on /).
