# Develop clever marketing campaigns for this hilarious and focused on getting sales

The full playbook is saved as **BaggageClaims_Marketing_Playbook_RevA.txt** — ten campaigns across four funnel stages, ~\$4,500 total spend, all wired to the kill/scale gates from the PRD. Here are the campaigns:

## Stage 1 — Attention (near-free)

**1. The Four Gates — unbranded viral drops.** Post the four launch-script verticals (FAT / OW / DARK / WHEE) raw, no logo, no link, at 6–8am commuter slots. The conversion trap is the comment bait: a pinned reply — *"comment FAT for where to get one"* — auto-DMs a one-field email capture via ManyChat. Any clip crossing 50k views in 72h gets the \$2k amplification budget. This IS the Phase 1 go/no-go gate.

**2. @TheBagComplains — the character account.** The bag runs its own social account and replies *in character* to viral airline-complaint posts: *"crew rested me on the WET tarmac?? sir I am LUGGAGE."* Zero ad spend — the cost is wit and speed (reply within 30 minutes of a meltdown post going viral). Every screenshot a travel account reposts is earned media.

**3. 100 Bags, 100 Reactions — creator seeding.** Ship 20 evaluation units to *mid-tier* travel comedians (50k–500k followers — 3× the engagement rate of mega-influencers for prop comedy), free unit + 10% affiliate code, one creative brief: "film the first genuine reaction." All 20 post in launch week so the algorithm reads "screaming suitcase" as a trend, not an ad. ~\$1.3k in COGS.

## Stage 2 — Interest → Capture

**4. The Lost Baggage Hotline — interactive microsite.** A fake customer-service flow where visitors "file a complaint on behalf of your bag": three questions, and the site renders a personalized audio complaint *in the bag's voice* plus a shareable claim ticket ("Claimant alleges being called FAT at Terminal C…"). Trading the download for an email targets a >35% capture rate, and the funniest submissions become your Phase 2 content.

**5. The Departure Board — referral program.** The email capture page is styled as an airport departure board and your boarding group *is* your referral rank: 3 referrals = free voice pack ("Group B"), 10 = \$19 Early Bird locked ("Group A"), 25 = collector Gold Voice tier ("First Class"). Goal: 3,000 emails pre-launch, which at a 2% list-to-backer conversion produces the day-one velocity spike that triggers Kickstarter's trending algorithm.

## Stage 3 — Conversion

**6. The Complaint Desk — live stunt (Austin home field).** A pop-up mock airline complaint desk, timed against SXSW. A uniformed "claims agent" takes real travelers' baggage horror stories; the demo unit reacts to each one live. Claimants leave with a printed ticket QR that unlocks a desk-only \$18 "Gate Agent Special" tier capped at 200 units. Budget ~\$300; the real prize is local news coverage — *"a suitcase filed a complaint at the airport today"* writes itself.

**7. The Kickstarter page as comedy architecture.** Reward tiers are cabin classes: **Checked** \$19 early bird → **Carry-On** \$24 → **Overhead Bin** \$34 (2-pack) → **First Class** \$49 collector (Gold Voice, signed, 100 cap — this exists to capture the 10% of backers who deliver 60% of funds) → **The Whole Cargo Hold** \$99 four-pack (your groomsmen/secret-Santa margin engine). Stretch goals are voice packs, never hardware — \$0.50/unit to fulfill, pure margin retention. FAQ stays in character: *"Q: Will TSA confiscate it? A: Alkaline AA. No lithium. We checked."*

**8. The Shame Ads — retargeting.** Five-dollar-test static ads to non-converting video viewers, written as five-star complaint reviews: *"My bag embarrassed me in front of the entire carousel. Five stars."* / *"TSA agent laughed so hard she forgot to swab my laptop. Five stars."* Kill if CAC exceeds \$10.

## Stage 4 — Retention flywheel

**9. Platinum Status — the DLC engine.** Every unit ships with a QR "frequent flyer card": register for a free first voice pack, then quarterly drops at \$7.99 (Holiday Heckler, Summer Breakdown, Bachelor Party Layover). The annual **Worst Flight Story** contest turns the winner's true story into a limited voice pack recorded by your talent — every entrant is UGC, every winner is press, and each cycle re-feeds Stage 1 for free.

**10. The Wholesale Trojan — self-demoing retail display.** For post-campaign airport gift retail: a counter display with a big red button that plays one random gag. It demos itself to people at the exact point of maximum baggage despair, at \$14.50 wholesale against a \$29.95 MSRP. The buyer pitch: *"the only product in your store that demos itself to customers who are already mad about bags."*

## The money logic

Every campaign routes to the same two gates: **kill** (Four Gates under 50k views and 500 emails in 30 days → stop, rework creative, don't fund production) and **scale** (2,000+ emails → green-light the 1,000-unit run). Total pre-revenue exposure stays ~\$4,500, with paid amplification only ever applied to clips that already proved themselves organically — the creative earns the right to spend, never the reverse.
