# BAGGAGE CLAIMS MK1 — Launch Video Script

**Format:** 2:15 Kickstarter hero cut + four 15-second vertical cuts (these verticals are the Phase 1 gate assets — the 50k-view test from the PRD happens with these, unbranded).

______________________________________________________________________

**COLD OPEN (0:00–0:08)**

- 0:00 — BLACK. Sound of a suitcase hitting a conveyor belt. Hard.
- 0:02 — **BAG (VO):** "OW!! — I felt that in my flip-flops!"
- 0:05 — CUT IN: tight on suitcase being yeeted onto a belt. Freeze frame.
- 0:06 — TITLE SMASH: **BAGGAGE CLAIMS.** Sub: *"Your luggage finally talks back."*
- 0:08 — HOST (VO): "Every year, 2.8 billion bags take a trip their owners never see."

**ACT 1 — THE SETUP (0:08–0:35)**

- 0:09 — Host packing at home; device straps onto handle in close-up. "So I built this. A tiny audio gadget that straps to any suitcase…"
- 0:18 — Split-screen montage: scale, handler toss, carousel. "It knows when it's being weighed. When it's being thrown. When it's been upside down in the dark for four hours."
- 0:27 — Host to lens, deadpan: "And it is NOT happy about it."
- 0:31 — TITLE: "MEET: BAGGAGE CLAIMS MK1" — device rotating on turntable.

**ACT 2 — THE MOMENTS (0:35–1:35)** — *each beat is also a standalone vertical cut*

- 0:36 — **Curb.** Host grabs handle. Beat. **BAG:** "Are you gonna buy me dinner first?" Bystander snorts. Super: `HANDLE_GRAB`
- 0:45 — **Check-in.** Agent lifts bag onto scale. Suspenseful stillness. Scale reads 62 lbs. **BAG:** "Are you… calling me FAT?" **Agent** (straight face): "Sir, the bag is over the limit." **Host:** "Take it up with her." Super: `WEIGH_IN`
- 0:58 — **The Toss (wide, filmed at distance).** Bag arcs cart-to-belt. Mid-air freeze: Wilhelm scream. THUD. **BAG:** "OW! Ow. Ow. Owww." Handler stops, genuinely confused, looks around. Super: `FREEFALL + IMPACT`
- 1:10 — **The Hold.** Black luggage cart, slow push-in. Nothing moves. **BAG** (sleepy): "Hello?… Anyone? …Cool, cool. I'll just be here. In the dark." Snoring. Super: `DARK_IDLE — 2 hrs`
- 1:20 — **Carousel.** Bag rounds the bend. **BAG** (delighted): "Wheeee! Again! Again!" Stranger at carousel: "Where did you GET that?" Super: `CAROUSEL`

**ACT 3 — THE PAYOFF (1:35–2:15)**

- 1:36 — Host, device in hand: "Three double-A batteries. No app. No pairing. One button. It just knows."
- 1:42 — Feature montage, lower thirds: `8 REAL TRAVEL MOMENTS — detected, not scripted` · `3 VOICE PACKS — Family / Salty / New Yorker` · `AA POWER — checked-bag legal. No lithium, no drama.` · `6 GAGS/HOUR MAX — funny, not annoying`
- 1:52 — Rapid gag reel: upside-down bag — "I'm inverted. Somebody call Goose." Belt rumble — "Oh sure, I'LL take the scenic route."
- 1:58 — **Host:** "Baggage Claims. Because somebody at the airport should be honest about what's happening to your stuff."
- 2:06 — **TAG CARD:** BAGGAGE CLAIMS MK1 — Kickstarter. *"Pledge. Pack. Let the bag complain."*
- 2:10 — **Post-tag button:** back at the scale. **BAG:** "Sixty-two pounds? That's mostly SOUVENIRS." **Host** (nods): "…She's not wrong." END.

## Production notes baked into the script

- **Every gag maps to a real classifier** — the supers (`WEIGH_IN`, `FREEFALL + IMPACT`) name the actual firmware event classes, so the video doubles as a technical demo. Backers who dig in find the PRD; everyone else just laughs.
- **The handler beat is the money shot.** Film at distance with a real handler's written consent, capture 3 takes of the confused-look-around — that clip (V2) is the likeliest viral breakout, and it's ranked \#1 in the gag catalog for a reason.
- **The four verticals run unbranded first.** Per the PRD gate: V1 "FAT", V2 "OW", V3 "DARK", V4 "WHEE" each carry their own text hook ("POV: your suitcase gets body-shamed at check-in"). Whichever one breaks 50k tells you which gag leads the hero cut.
- **Compliance footer on the end card** keeps the airline-optics risk inoculated from day one: *"Prototype shown. Alkaline AA only. Switch OFF before checking per airline policy."*
- **Hero VO is final contracted talent, not scratch** — the audio cue sheet in the file maps every line to the microSD numbering (0001–0009) already defined in the assembly guide, so the same clips you record for the video ship on the product.
