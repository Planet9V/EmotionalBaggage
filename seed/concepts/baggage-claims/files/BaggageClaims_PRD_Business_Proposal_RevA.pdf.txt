3. Core Technology

Three mature, commodity building blocks carry the product:

3.1 Sensing - MPU-6050 6-axis MEMS IMU. 16-bit accel (+/-8g covers drop peaks) + gyro (+/-500 dps covers
carousel rotation) at 100 Hz with 44 Hz anti-alias filtering per ICD sec 2.3. $0.90 in volume.

3.2 Classification - rule-based feature engine on ESP32-S3. Ten-second rolling history; features (magnitude, jerk,
tilt, gravity-removed axes) feed eight rule classifiers: hard impact, free-fall, weigh-in hold signature, carry tilt, carousel
period detection via gyro zero-crossings, conveyor rumble variance, inverted orientation, dark-idle timeout. A governor
(per-class latches, 6 gags/hour cap, 3 s cooldown) keeps the toy charming rather than annoying - the single most
important UX line in the firmware.

3.3 Audio - YX5200/DFPlayer-class MP3 decoder. Hardware decode from microSD, 3W amp, 9600-baud UART
control. 128 clips x 3 voice packs (Family / Salty / New Yorker) on a $2 card - content ships independently of firmware,
enabling DLC and localization with zero board respins.

Power strategy: 3xAA alkaline; deep sleep ~30-60 uA with 1 s wake poll + latched IMU motion interrupt = 3-6 months
per battery set. Alkaline is the compliance hinge: no UN38.3, no FAA lithium restrictions, no accidental-activation
debate.

4. System Architecture

Single-board device, radios disabled (FCC Part 15 unintentional-radiator scope only; the ESP32-S3 module itself is
certified). Data flow: IMU 100 Hz > ring buffer > feature extractor > FSM
(BOOT/CALIBRATE/IDLE/ACTIVE_SAMPLE/CLASSIFY/DISPATCH/COOLDOWN) > UART > DFPlayer > 8-ohm

6. Firmware Deliverable

Delivered as BaggageClaims_Classifier_RevA.ino (262 lines; Arduino-ESP32 core 3.x + DFRobotDFPlayerMini
library). Module map:

Module | Responsibility | Key constants
mpuInit / mpuSample | Raw I2C per ICD register plan (100 Hz, DLPF 44 Hz, +/-8g, latched motion INT) | 0x1C=0x10, 0x1B=0x08
ringPush / ringVarMag | 10 s circular history; variance windows 0.5 s / 8 s | N_HIST=1000
classify() | 8 rule classifiers on gravity-removed axes; serial event logging | IMPACT_G=8.0, STILL_VAR=0.002
dispatch() | Governor: class latch, 3 s cooldown, 6/hour cap, BUSY gate | COOLDOWN_MS, MAX_GAGS_HOUR=6
serviceButton() | Tap = volume 15/23/30, hold = voice pack, 10 s = reset | debounce 30 ms
maybeSleep() | 1 s light-sleep poll; latched INT survives; ext1 GPIO wake in Rev B | SLEEP_AFTER 5 min

Rev A caveats (flagged in code comments): gyro-axis-to-carousel mapping must be verified against final mount
orientation; battery ADC divider is Rev B hardware.

assemblies at 1,000 pcs, $1-5 per board at high volume, 2-layer PCB $0.50-1.50 at 1k+.

12. Risks & Mitigations

Risk | Severity | Mitigation
Lithium-in-checked-bags optics | High | Alkaline-only by design + compliance card: switch OFF before checking; carry-on mode in marketing
Annoyance / airline complaints | Medium | Governed output: 6/hour cap, 3 s cooldown, 85 dBA ceiling, volume button
Imitators | Medium | Speed + brand + VO talent; design patent + trademark; classifier as trade secret
Voice rights / publicity claims | Medium | Contracted original VO with releases; no celebrity impressions
Seasonality (Q4-heavy) | Low-Med | Voice-pack DLC; spring-break/summer editions; wedding/bachelor gag angle
Single-source codec clones | Low | Dual-source YX5200/KT148A; SPI flash path removes module dependency in Rev C
